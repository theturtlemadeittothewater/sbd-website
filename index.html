Six layers of intelligence, running on every client, every day.

Most client success systems just report data, that's it. They show you what's already happened, but they don't give you any insight on what to do with it. Kairos™ watches what your clients do, decides what should happen next, sends that work to the right person, and gets smarter the longer it runs.

Sees

Live signal on every client, at all times.

Kairos™ pulls together the inputs that actually matter: engagement, progress, payments, key touchpoints. No more guessing from scattered check-ins and Stripe exports. You open it and know, in one view, who's thriving, who's drifting, and where the program itself might be failing people.

Decides

Automatically assigns the highest value task.

Kairos™ looks at that signal and identifies the single highest leverage action for each client today, with the reasoning attached. Your team stops asking "who should I focus on?" and starts each day with a short list of prioritized moves grounded in data.

Routes

The right action lands in the right person's hands.

Each role gets its own Today queue, sorted by leverage. Big conversations and program fixes go to the Founder. Re-engagement and support go to the CSMs. System hygiene goes to the operator. This eliminates endless Slack messages, useless meetings, and disorganized spreadsheets keeping you from making more money.

Sequences

One continuous lifecycle in a single system.

Onboarding, delivery, and backend sales all run on one pipeline. Kairos™ knows where each client is in their journey and what's going to drive them to a case study and upsell the quickest. Nothing slips through the cracks, enabling your team to turn their brain off and do what the system tells them to do to make you the most cash.

Compounds

Client wins become growth assets by default.

When a client gets a win, most teams say "that's awesome!" and move on. Kairos™ turns that moment into money making moves: the renewal, the referral, the testimonial, the case study. The same work that delivers results is what drives more demand and more revenue.

Learns

The system gets smarter the longer it runs.

Kairos™ runs a quarterly analysis of all the data that's been produced inside of the system. It then learns what worked and what didn't and automatically adjusts the system to maximize the amount of revenue your client success department produces. Keeping your business up to date.

How we turn client success into profit

Coaching is behavior change. The primary mechanism of behavior change is the environment. Not motivation, discipline, or accountability.

When clients don't do the work, it's because the program made doing the right thing the exception and the wrong thing the default option of the program.

Our method engineers the highest value client experience where the default path results in clients actually doing the work, continuing to work with you, buying again, and referring their friends to the program.

Phase 1

Audit

We critically analyze every component of your business to determine who your highest value clients actually are, what the true constraint is for each area of your business, and set the foundations for your custom tailored solutions.

Phase 2

Design the highest value client experience

Engineer the highest value client experience that minimizes time-to-value and maximizes lifetime value, making results, referrals, and backend sales inevitable. This includes designing and building a world class onboarding experience that sets clear expectations, eliminates buyer's remorse, and gets clients to ROI as fast as possible.

Phase 3

Install Kairos™

A combination of milestone and time-based pipelines that move clients through your program automatically, monitor their progress, identify at-risk clients before they become a problem, and drive conversion opportunities for backend sales. Eliminates the guesswork for your CS team with automated task assignment based on where clients are and what they need.

Phase 4

Client success training

Train your team to deliver transformational coaching that gets clients to confront what they're avoiding and do the work without hand-holding. Battle tested systems that have handled every niche and the largest client load in the entire info-product industry. Elite group call methods that deliver 1:1 level value at scale, without hiring and managing a large CS team.

Phase 5

Backend revenue architecture

Implement the systematic path that turns one-time buyers into lifelong clients worth 10x their initial purchase. Renewals, cross sells, down sells, upsells, masterminds, and other offers that only become sustainably possible after clients get results from your core program.
